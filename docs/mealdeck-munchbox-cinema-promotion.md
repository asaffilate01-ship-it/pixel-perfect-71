# MealDeck Munchbox cinema prize draw — design draft

## Brand and prize
- Use the actual approved stacked-card MealDeck logo via the existing MealDeckLogo component; never recreate the logo.
- Film title and cinema name appear as ordinary text identifying the prize. No Marvel, Disney, Avengers or Cineworld logos, characters, posters or official type treatment. No suggestion of sponsorship.
- One cinema ticket: Avengers: Doomsday, Cineworld Luton, Wednesday 16 December 2026, 7:20pm (screening details supplied by the owner).
- Entry window: 3–6 December 2026 inclusive, UK time, qualifying registered customer website purchases.

## Draft status / release gate
The component is not mounted on any public route. It is a responsive presentation draft only. Before publishing, confirm the ticket is secured and transferable, eligible Munchbox item IDs, age/film admission conditions, exact eligible transaction channels, number of entries per account, refund/cancellation treatment, random draw date and auditable selection, contact and 24-hour response/collection rules, full terms and privacy notice. Winner filming must be voluntary and separately consented to. Marketing consent is not required to enter. Do not promise automatic entry or publish the promotion until eligibility records can be reliably reconciled against paid member orders. Check the proposed promotion against UK advertising/prize-draw requirements and cinema ticket conditions.

## Layout
Desktop: approved logo and generic cinema-ticket icon at left, copy and CTAs at right. Tablet/mobile: compact logo row followed by legible live text and wrapping/full-width CTAs. No flashing animation, no raster text, no third-party artwork, no obstructive popup. Verify 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px viewports and keyboard focus before launch.
