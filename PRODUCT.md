## Group ordering rooms and payment choices

The public `/group-order` flow uses a server-backed room for up to ten people, including its organiser. The organiser creates the room and shares private invitations. Each person accepts, joins and chooses their own dishes, flavours and extras from the same five confirmed menus, then marks their choices ready. The server expires invitations that have not been accepted within three minutes. People who already joined are not silently dropped while choosing or paying.

The organiser reviews and finalises the complete current room after all remaining people are ready and no invitations are pending. The server validates and prices the complete order, freezes each person's choices and assigns their food to a named numbered bag. Each person's short name remains beside their exact items on the bill, kitchen display and receipt so packing can keep everyone’s order separate. Attached extras follow their parent quantities. The ordinary personal basket remains separate.

New rooms offer two payment modes: each person pays for their own food, or the organiser pays for everyone. Billing stays itemised per person in either case, using the canonical amounts and whole-order discount allocation. Picks and shares cannot change after freezing. Only full verified funding sends one complete master order to the kitchen; partial payments never create separate kitchen orders. Existing payer-owned sessions resume without replacing unknown payment attempts. Staff acceptance and printing remain separate from payment confirmation.

This supersedes the earlier local invitation/return-link planner and the equal-split choice in that checkout flow. Historical drafts, unnamed bag receipts and existing payment records retain their compatibility and recovery rules; they do not define the new room experience. Room availability still depends on installed schema, configured merchant integration, kitchen readiness and member/guest eligibility. See `docs/mealdeck-group-ordering.md` and `docs/mealdeck-shared-payments.md`.

## WhatsApp enquiry contract

The optional MealDeck handoff prepares **dish names, quantities, modifiers, location and requested fulfilment only**. It must not send the customer's phone, address, account data, prices, discounts, payment state or access tier in a URL. The official business number is supplied by server-only location configuration after operations approval. The chat is an **enquiry**, not an order.

Future automated agents require scoped, explicit tools; tenant/location routing; current server-read pricing; safe modifier selection; idempotent checkout; verified payment webhooks; and handoff to humans for ambiguity, allergens or unsupported requests. Agent text cannot confer order authority.
